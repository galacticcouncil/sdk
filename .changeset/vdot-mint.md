Mint vDOT from DOT and supply it to the money market in one signature

- DOT travels through Asset Hub to Bifrost, which mints vDOT and sends it back
- The vDOT is supplied to the money market on the signer's behalf
- Fees for each hop are quoted live; whatever a hop leaves over is refunded
