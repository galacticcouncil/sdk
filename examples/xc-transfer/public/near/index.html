NTT — NEAR testnet → Hydration

The NEAR half is the live testnet deployment. Hydration is not on wormhole testnet, so its leg exists only on a chopsticks fork: validations reading it fail against the mainnet rpc, and a signed vaa is delivered with whm's _probeNearNttDelivery.ts - the command is printed once the testnet guardian signs.

Connect wallet lists the NEAR wallets with testnet support and Ethereum wallets: MetaMask signs for the NEAR account with the same 0x… id, which has to hold testnet NEAR for gas and deposits. An Ethereum wallet approves one action at a time, so the native NEAR route asks three times; wNEAR once.
