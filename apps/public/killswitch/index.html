XCM Kill Switch

Any single Technical Committee seat can stop all XCM, inbound and outbound, in one call. Resuming, or switching one side only, takes a committee motion passed by a two-thirds super-majority. A seat held by an EVM account signs through the dispatch precompile (Rabby, MetaMask, Trezor through Rabby); a substrate seat signs with its extension.

Kill switch

Suspends both sides in the block it lands in. Outbound transfers fail and roll back; inbound messages stay queued and run once execution resumes. One seat can throw it, only a motion undoes it.
