TC Voting

Technical Committee votes from a seat held by an EVM account. Such an account has no substrate key, so every call goes through the EVM dispatch precompile, wrapped in Dispatcher.dispatch_with_extra_gas since collective calls are operational. Sign with Rabby or MetaMask, Trezor through Rabby. To submit a proposal, use Dispatch.

Open proposals

Close executes the proposal once the threshold is met, or rejects it after the voting period ends. Every call is decoded back against the live metadata before the wallet is asked to sign.
